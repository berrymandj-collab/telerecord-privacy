Privacy Policy for TeleRecord
Last updated: May 18, 2026

TeleRecord ("the App") is a teleprompter and video recording application
developed by Dennis Berryman. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles
your information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

TeleRecord does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.
The App has no user accounts, no registration, and no connection to any server
controlled by the developer.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All data created within the App — including scripts, settings, and recorded
videos — is stored exclusively on your device. This data never leaves your
device unless you explicitly choose to share or export it yourself.

  • Scripts and settings: stored in your device's local app storage.
  • Recorded videos: saved to your device's local storage or gallery when
    you choose to save them.

3. Permissions Used

The App requests the following device permissions solely to provide its core
functionality:

  • Camera: required to record video.
  • Microphone: required to record audio and to use the speech-to-text
    dictation feature in the script editor.
  • Storage / Media: required to save recorded videos to your device gallery.

These permissions are used only while you are actively using the App and are
never used to collect data about you.

4. Third-Party Services

TeleRecord does not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks,
crash-reporting services, or tracking SDKs. No data is sent to any third party.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a future version of the App, the updated policy will
be made available at the same URL and the "Last updated" date above will be
revised.
